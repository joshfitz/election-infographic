SELECTABLE TEXT VERSION

What each vote combination does

Issue 8 changes the nine seats to district-only elections. Issue 9 creates nine district seats and four citywide seats, phased in across staggered elections.

Council result for every combination of votes on Issues 8 and 9
Issue 8Issue 9Result
NONO9 members. All voters elect all nine seats over staggered elections.
YESNO9 district members. You vote for your district seat when it is up.
NOYES9 district + 4 citywide. 11 members beginning in 2028; 13 beginning in 2030.
YESYESSame structure as NO / YES. Both amendments take effect, with no additional structural change.

Under Issue 9, the first two citywide seats are elected in 2027 and the next two in 2029. A 2027 commission draws new district boundaries for use in the 2029 elections.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the most common questions about how the two amendments interact.

What's the difference between Columbus Issues 8 and 9?

Both proposals would let voters in each of Columbus's nine council districts elect their own district representative. Issue 8 would keep City Council at nine members, while Issue 9 would add four members elected citywide, expanding Council to 13 members by 2030.

What happens if both Issues 8 and 9 pass?

Both amendments would take effect. Columbus would elect nine district representatives exclusively through district voters and add four citywide representatives, creating a 13-member council by 2030. The same council structure would result if Issue 9 passed alone.

Does Issue 9 replace Issue 8?

No. Issue 9 incorporates Issue 8's district-election changes and adds further reforms, including four at-large seats and an earlier redistricting process. The proposals were designed to work together, so approving both would not cancel either amendment.

How many Columbus City Council members would there be?

Columbus currently has nine council members. If only Issue 8 passes, that number stays at nine; if Issue 9 passes, Council expands to 11 members in 2028 and 13 in 2030. If neither passes, Council remains at nine members.

What happens to at-large voting?

Currently, every Columbus voter can vote in elections for all nine council seats, even though candidates must live in their respective districts. Under Issue 8 alone, voters would elect only their district representative; under Issue 9, they would elect their district representative and participate in elections for four at-large representatives, with elections staggered across different years.
