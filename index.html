HOW IT WORKS TODAY

9 districts. 9 members.
Everyone votes for all 9.

Members must live in the districts they represent, but every Columbus voter can vote in every council race.

RESIDENCYOne member must live in each district.
ELECTIONEvery voter chooses among candidates for all nine seats.

WHY TWO PROPOSALS?

2025

District 7 voters preferred one candidate; the citywide vote elected another.

2026

Our City, Our Say advanced Issue 8. After compromise talks did not produce one amendment, City Council proposed Issue 9.

THE TRADEOFF

D

Neighborhood accountabilityDistrict residents choose their representative.

C

Citywide accountabilityEvery voter helps choose members with a citywide constituency.

Neither structure guarantees better government.

OTHER CHANGES

Both: district members are recalled by their district's voters. Issue 9: district candidate petitions drop from 250 to 150 signatures; the maximum population gap between the largest and smallest district rises from 1% to 7.5%; council voting thresholds adjust as seats are added.

The choice is about who elects whom - and to whom elected officials are accountable.

NONPARTISAN VOTER INFORMATION This guide does not endorse either proposal.
